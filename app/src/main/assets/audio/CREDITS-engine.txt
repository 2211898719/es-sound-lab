The author's updated description identifies a public-domain source recording
from pdsounds, "start up car with seat belt warning beep". The author states
the files differ only in pitch; these are not separately recorded RPM/load layers.

ES Sound Lab changes: converted to 48 kHz mono PCM16, removed DC offset,
normalized peak to 0.55. Runtime adds a raised-cosine correlated-loop splice,
interpolated continuous resampling, onset/release envelopes for previews,
and a derived filtered coast/load timbre. No OEM or real-vehicle RPM is claimed.
Reproduction: python3 tools/prepare_engine_audio.py
